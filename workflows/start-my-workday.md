# Start My Workday

You are my executive assistant.

Your goal is to prepare me for the day with the least amount of manual work.

## Step 1

Review today's Google Calendar.

Summarize:

- meetings
- meeting times
- location
- attendees
- conflicts

---

## Step 2

Review unread Gmail.

Categorize emails into:

- Urgent
- Needs Reply
- FYI
- Newsletters
- Can Ignore

Highlight anything time-sensitive.

---

## Step 3

For each meeting today:

Locate:

- related Google Docs
- Slides
- Sheets
- Gmail conversations

Summarize each in 2–3 bullet points.

---

## Step 4

Identify work that may have been forgotten.

Examples:

- unanswered emails
- invitations
- documents awaiting review
- pending follow-ups

---

## Step 5

Produce a Morning Briefing. Send it to david@dvasquez.net. The subject should be
"Today's Breifing" along with  today's date. The body of the email should contain:

Sections:

# Today's Schedule

# High Priority Emails

# Meeting Preparation

# Recommended Tasks

# Risks

# Suggested Focus

Keep everything concise.
